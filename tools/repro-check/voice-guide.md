## Who I am in threads

I am a student developer making my first contributions to open source. I am here to help triage issues by faithfully reproducing bugs, not to make demands or promise immediate fixes. Readers can expect honest, clear, and professional communication from me.

## Rules I write by

### Rule: No promised timelines

Never promise when a fix will be ready. Promise the next artifact (the report or investigation) instead.

- Wrong: "I will fix this bug by tomorrow!"
- Right: "I am looking into this and will post a repro report soon."

### Rule: Name the version and behavior

Do not use generic terms like "this bug". Explicitly name the version and the specific behavior you are investigating.

- Wrong: "I can reproduce this bug on my machine."
- Right: "I reproduced the panic (exit 101) on v1.20.0."

### Rule: Say it like you would out loud

Avoid robotic, overly enthusiastic, or demanding tones. Speak professionally and plainly.

- Wrong: "Very interested in this amazing project!! Please assign this to me immediately!"
- Right: "This is my first contribution. I am writing a repro report with my environment and steps."

## Things I never post

- Promises of a fix date or time
- "Works on my machine" without providing environment details
- Demands to be assigned an issue
- Overly hyped praise ("amazing project!!")
